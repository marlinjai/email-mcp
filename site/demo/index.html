          Worth knowing

          Credentials
          Stored in ~/.email-mcp on your machine, AES-256-GCM encrypted with a key derived from the machine.
          OAuth
          Gmail and Outlook use a shared app by default. Set EMAIL_MCP_GMAIL_CLIENT_ID to bring your own.
          Gmail scope
          The default permits permanent deletion. Choose restricted during setup to drop it.
          Assistant

          Ready when you are. One command adds your accounts in the terminal, then point your client at the server and the tools show up.

          No account with us, no dashboard, no lock-in. Uninstall by removing the npm package; your accounts and tokens stay in ~/.email-mcp.
